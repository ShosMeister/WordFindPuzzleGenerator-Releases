# Word Find Puzzle Generator

A free Windows app for making printable word-find (word search) puzzles from your own word lists.

**[⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/ShosMeister/WordFindPuzzleGenerator-Releases/releases/latest)**

---

## Features

- **Your words, your puzzle.** Type a word list or load one from a text file. Multi-word entries such as `ICE CREAM` are supported.
- **Puzzle profiles.** Pick Beginner, Easy, Classic, Challenge, or Discovery, or choose your own directions (Custom).
- **Word directions.** Horizontal, vertical, diagonal down, and diagonal up, each forward and/or reverse.
- **Two puzzle styles.**
  - *Standard*: words may touch and share letters, like most newspaper puzzles.
  - *Clean*: words never touch, so they're easier to find. Needs a larger board.
- **Size guidance.** The app suggests a board size for your list and warns you before generating if the board looks too small.
- **Missing-word highlighting.** Any word that didn't fit is highlighted in red in your list, so nothing is left out without you knowing.
- **Printing.**
  - Full-page or 4×6 card formats, with 1, 2, or 4 puzzles per page.
  - Optional solution page.
  - Title, author, and footer text.
  - Decorative borders, or your own images.
- **Save and reopen** puzzles as `.wfp` files.

## Download and install

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/ShosMeister/WordFindPuzzleGenerator-Releases/releases/latest) and download `WordFindPuzzleGenerator-<version>-win-x64.zip`.
2. Unzip it to any folder.
3. Run `WordfindPuzzleGenerator.exe`. Keep the `Resources` folder next to it, because it holds the print decorations.

No installer and no .NET download needed. Everything is included.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** The app isn't code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you the first time you run it. Click **More info → Run anyway**. You can confirm your download matches the published file using the SHA256 checksum listed on each release.

## Quick start

1. Enter your words in **Word List**, one per line, or click **Load Word File**.
2. Choose a **Puzzle Profile** and a **Puzzle Style**.
3. Set **Rows** and **Columns**. The **Suggested Size** shows what will comfortably fit.
4. Click **Generate Puzzle**.
5. Click **Print Preview** to set up printing, or **Save Puzzle** to keep it.

**Tip:** if words are highlighted in red, they didn't fit. Make the board bigger, allow more directions, or switch to Standard style. A highlighted word isn't necessarily the problem: a longer word placed earlier may have taken its space.
